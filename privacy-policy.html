Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

1. Introduction & Data Controller

Toaleta ("the App") is developed and operated by Toaleta App (toaleta.app@gmail.com) ("we", "us", "the Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Data Controller: Toaleta App (toaleta.app@gmail.com)
Contact: toaleta.app@gmail.com
Jurisdiction: Romania, European Union

2. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

We process your data based on:

  • Legitimate interest — to provide the core toilet-finding service (Art. 6(1)(f))
  • Consent — for voluntary contributions such as photos, reviews, and toilet listings (Art. 6(1)(a))
  • Contract performance — to deliver the service you purchased (Art. 6(1)(b))

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Location Data

We access your device's location only while the App is in active use ("When In Use" permission). This data is used to display nearby toilets, calculate distances, and provide directions. Your last known location is temporarily cached on your device to improve the App experience on next launch. We do not maintain a history of your movements and do not transmit your location to our servers except as part of queries to third-party mapping services (see Section 7).

3.2 User-Generated Content

When you voluntarily contribute to the App, we collect:

  • Photos you upload of toilet facilities (max 3 per toilet, max 1 MB each)
  • Ratings (overall, cleanliness, accessibility, privacy, smell, condition) and written reviews (max 500 characters)
  • Toilet listings you create (location coordinates, name, attributes such as pricing, accessibility features, amenities, opening hours)
  • Reports you submit about toilets (reason and optional description)

3.3 Anonymous Authentication

We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to assign a unique anonymous identifier (UID) to your device. This UID is attached to your contributions to prevent abuse and allow you to manage your submissions. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.

3.4 Device Data Stored Locally

The App stores the following data on your device only:

  • Onboarding completion status
  • Language preference (Romanian/English)
  • Theme preference (System/Light/Dark)
  • Last known latitude and longitude
  • Favorite toilets list (stored locally, not uploaded)
  • Cached toilet data for offline access
  • Pending offline actions queue (submissions made without internet, synced when connectivity is restored)
  • Submission rate limiting data (daily counts and timestamps to prevent spam)

This data never leaves your device except when you explicitly submit contributions.

3.5 Crash Reports

The App includes Firebase Crashlytics, which may automatically collect anonymous crash reports (stack traces, device model, OS version) to help us fix bugs. No personally identifiable information is included in crash reports.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identification (name, email, phone, social accounts)
  • Browsing history or in-app usage analytics
  • Contacts, calendar, health, or other device data
  • Financial or payment information (purchases are processed entirely by Apple)
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Background location data

5. Payment Processing

The App is available as a one-time purchase on the Apple App Store. All payment processing is handled exclusively by Apple Inc. We never receive, process, or store your payment information, credit card details, or Apple ID.

6. Photo Handling & Privacy

  • GPS metadata (EXIF) and camera metadata are automatically stripped from all photos before upload to protect your location privacy
  • Photos are compressed (max 1 MB) and stored on Firebase Cloud Storage
  • Uploaded photos are accessible via URL within the App to other users
  • Photos are used exclusively to help the community assess toilet facilities
  • You may request removal of your photos by contacting us with your anonymous user ID

7. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — anonymous authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Storage for photos, Crashlytics for crash reporting
    Privacy Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • OpenStreetMap (via Overpass API) — public toilet location data. Your approximate location (coordinates and search radius) is sent to Overpass API servers to retrieve nearby toilet data
    Privacy Policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
  • Apple MapKit — location search (geocoding) and walking directions. Search queries and approximate location context are sent to Apple
    Privacy Policy: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • CARTO — map tile rendering via MapLibre. Map tile requests include the visible map region
    Privacy Policy: carto.com/privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

8. International Data Transfers

Firebase services (Google LLC) may process data on servers located outside the European Economic Area, including the United States. Google provides appropriate safeguards for such transfers under EU Standard Contractual Clauses. All data transmitted between the App and third-party services is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

9. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • All network communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • EXIF metadata is stripped from photos before upload
  • Firebase security rules restrict unauthorized data access
  • Anonymous authentication prevents personal data exposure
  • Local data is stored in the App's sandboxed storage

10. Data Retention

  • Your contributions (reviews, photos, toilet listings) are retained as long as they remain useful to the community
  • Anonymous authentication identifiers are retained to maintain contribution history and prevent abuse
  • Crash reports are retained for 90 days
  • Local device data (cache, favorites) is retained until you clear it via Settings or delete the App
  • You may request deletion of all your cloud-stored data at any time (see Section 11)

11. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access — obtain a copy of all data associated with your anonymous identifier
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate information in your contributions
  • Erasure — request deletion of all your contributions and associated data
  • Restriction — request that we limit processing of your data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw consent for voluntary contributions at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at toaleta.app@gmail.com with your anonymous user ID (found in the Settings tab of the App). We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Romanian Data Protection Authority (ANSPDCP):
Website: dataprotection.ro
Email: anspdcp@dataprotection.ro

12. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours as required by GDPR Article 33. If the breach poses a high risk to your rights, we will also notify affected users through the App or other available means.

13. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 16 years of age (in accordance with Romanian GDPR implementation, Law 190/2018). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

14. Network Connectivity

The App monitors your device's network connectivity status (WiFi, cellular, or offline) solely to manage offline functionality and queue submissions for later sync. No connectivity data is transmitted externally.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the App. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
